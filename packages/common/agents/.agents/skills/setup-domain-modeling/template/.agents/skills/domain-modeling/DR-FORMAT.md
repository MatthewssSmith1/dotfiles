# Decision Record Format

Files use `NNNN-short-slug.md`. Scan the relevant journal for its highest number and increment it.

A single-paragraph record is valid. Record that a decision was made and why rather than filling out sections.

Include these only when they add genuine value:

- **Status** frontmatter (`proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by NNNN`) when decisions may be revisited.
- **Considered Options** when rejected alternatives are worth remembering.
- **Consequences** when non-obvious downstream effects need to be recorded.

---

## Template

```md
# {Short title of the decision}

{One to three sentences covering the context, decision, and reason.}
```
